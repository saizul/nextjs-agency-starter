Structured data helps search engines understand your pages. It does not guarantee rich results, but it removes ambiguity.

## Worth adding

- **Organization** on the home page, with your name, logo and URL.
- **WebSite** to describe the site and its language.
- **BreadcrumbList** on nested pages so the hierarchy is clear.
- **Article** on blog posts, with dates and an author.
- **FAQPage** only where the questions and answers are visible on the page.
- **Service** on pages that describe a service you sell.

## Usually skip

Review markup for your own business, fake ratings and anything that does not appear on the page. It breaks guidelines and risks a manual action.

## Implementation tip

Generate JSON-LD from the same data that renders the page. That keeps the markup honest and removes a whole class of drift bugs.

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "BreadcrumbList",
  "itemListElement": []
}
```

Validate with a testing tool after each change, and keep the builders covered by unit tests.
