Technical SEO sounds intimidating, but most small sites lose traffic to the same handful of problems. Work through this list once a quarter and you will catch the majority of them.

## Crawling and indexing

Search engines can only rank what they can reach. Confirm these first:

- `robots.txt` does not block pages you want in results.
- Every important page appears in the XML sitemap and returns a `200` status.
- Pages you do not want indexed use `noindex`, not just a robots rule.
- There is one canonical URL per page, and it points to itself.

## Metadata

Each page needs a unique title and description that match what the page offers. Keep titles under roughly 60 characters so they are not cut off.

```html
<title>Technical SEO audits | Acme Digital</title>
<meta name="description" content="Find and fix what holds your site back in search." />
<link rel="canonical" href="https://example.com/services/technical-seo" />
```

## Structure and links

Use one `h1` per page and a logical heading order. Link to important pages from other relevant pages with descriptive anchor text, because "click here" tells a crawler nothing.

## Speed and stability

Measure real-user data where possible. Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint and Cumulative Layout Shift are the metrics to watch.

## What to do next

Fix the problems that affect many pages first, then the ones on your highest-value pages. Re-crawl, compare, and keep a short changelog so you can connect improvements to results.
