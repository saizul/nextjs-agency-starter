This is a fictional example included to show how a case study can be structured.

## The challenge

The client ran a catalogue site on an ageing platform. Pages were slow, URLs were inconsistent and every release risked a drop in search traffic.

## What we did

1. Mapped every legacy URL to its new equivalent and tested the redirects in bulk.
2. Rebuilt templates with static generation and optimised images.
3. Added structured data for products, breadcrumbs and the organisation.
4. Monitored indexation daily for the first month after launch.

## Outcome

Traffic recovered within two weeks and then grew steadily. The team now publishes new category pages without waiting for a developer.
