This is a fictional example included to show how a case study can be structured.

## The challenge

The company had a blog with scattered topics and no clear link to the product. Traffic was low quality and rarely converted.

## What we did

1. Grouped search demand by intent and picked topics close to the product.
2. Wrote briefs so any writer could deliver consistent articles.
3. Built topic hubs with deliberate internal links.
4. Refreshed old posts instead of letting them decay.

## Outcome

Fewer, stronger pages brought more of the right visitors, and the editorial rhythm became sustainable for a small team.
